Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

BAROK EDUCATION PRO is a learning website for Grades 9–12. This page explains the information the website is designed to use. The website operator should review and update this policy before opening registration to the public.

Information collected

When a student registers, the website requests a name, email address and grade. Firebase Authentication is used to manage sign-in credentials. The website does not need a student's home address, phone number, precise location, government identification number or payment-card information.

How information is used

Account information is used to provide sign-in, display a student profile and associate learning progress or published results with the correct account. Do not submit sensitive personal information in course forms, lesson content or messages.

Service providers and storage

The website uses Google Firebase services for authentication, database storage and hosting. Those services process information according to Google's applicable privacy terms and settings.

Children and student accounts

Students under 18 should use the website with the knowledge and guidance of a parent, guardian or school. The operator must confirm the applicable consent and student-data requirements before public launch.

Security and access

Access to student records and results should be restricted through Firebase Security Rules. Never share passwords, verification codes or private account credentials. Report suspected account or data issues to the website operator through the contact channel provided by the school or operator.

Retention and requests

The operator should establish a retention schedule and a verified contact channel for account-access, correction and deletion requests before public launch. Account deletion should be handled through a verified process that also considers related student records and applicable school requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website changes. The operator should publish the effective date of each update.
